## Overview
Kim Holden, Principal Deputy Associate Director for Workforce Policy and Innovation at OPM, discusses recruitment, hiring and assessment with the course host, including the Merit Hiring Plan and common hiring myths.

## Key points
- Merit system principles, prohibited personnel practices and veterans' preference distinguish federal hiring from the private sector [02:01]. Positions are position-based, not person-based. [04:05]
- Excepted service jobs need not be posted on USAJOBS, but Holden advises advertising them. [13:25]
- The Merit Hiring Plan, a joint OPM and OMB memo, phases out self-rated occupational questionnaires [07:11], sets a two-page resume for GS and SES applicants, and requires agency talent teams staffed with I-O psychologists and HR specialists. [08:14]
- Holden calls hiring an executive's most important job [10:19]. Executives should know the few hiring authorities they need [12:22] and, when HR says no, ask why or why not. [16:34]
- Managers own the job analysis; HR supplies templates. [17:36]
- Active recruiting options include hiring fairs with tentative offers under direct hire authority and apprenticeships [19:40], upskilling details [20:42], USAJOBS resume mining (including shared military spouse resumes) and video in job announcements. [21:43]
- Self-ratings tend to inflate [25:48]. Skills-based assessments, multiple hurdles and structured interviews show what candidates can actually do. [28:55]
- In a segment on myths from an OPM myths-versus-facts guide, the speakers say managers should be involved [29:56] and may recruit actively [30:58], and SMEs may help rate and rank [32:59]. Current SES members can be considered noncompetitively without rewriting ECQs. [34:01]

## Executive takeaways
- Base hiring decisions on job analysis, skills-based assessments and structured interviews, within merit rules. (ECQ3 Merit & Competence)
- Treat hiring as a core executive duty done in partnership with HR, and treat retention check-ins the same way. (ECQ4 Leading People)
- Use skills-focused assessments and SME help with rating so certificates hold qualified applicants; Holden says weak certificates send the hiring action back to the start. (ECQ2 Driving Efficiency)
