## Overview
OPM Director Scott Kupor and Federal CIO Greg Barbaccia argue that AI is now mature enough for broad government use and that senior executives are expected to apply it. They cover the AI Action Plan, data and governance prerequisites, risk culture, workforce effects and early use cases.

## Key points
- Barbaccia says AI is now commoditized because compute infrastructure matured; it is faster and better but not yet cheaper. [03:04]
- The AI Action Plan is described as whole-of-nation: infrastructure, less burdensome regulation, education, energy and skilled trades. [07:07]
- Kupor links AI-driven efficiency to the SES ECQs and performance reviews. [11:12]
- People should focus on work that needs human intuition or accountability; AI supports decisions rather than owning them. [15:17]
- Start by mapping bottlenecks, outputs, customers and service design. Data quality, access control, audit logging and data tagging come before agentic workflows. [23:23]
- Barbaccia says systems already accredited for PII are the base for AI deployment; executives should learn appropriate use from their Chief AI Officer and compliance staff. [25:24]
- The speakers call for small, hypothesis-driven experiments and say no initiative can be fully de-risked. [28:26]
- Faster adoption paths named: FedRAMP prioritization of large AI vendors, ATO process changes, GSA's USAi platform and OneGov low-cost pilots. [30:26] [31:26]
- Mature use cases include translation, topic clustering and public comment analysis with human final review; agentic AI could lead call-center contact under human supervision. [39:37] [41:39]

## Executive takeaways
- Treat AI adoption as an efficiency obligation and measure it against a baseline. (ECQ2 Driving Efficiency)
- Upskill and redeploy staff whose tasks are automated; find better roles for strong performers instead of exiting them. (ECQ4 Leading People)
- Contain risk in small pilots with clear stop rules instead of waiting for zero risk. (ECQ5 Achieving Results)
- Build a working relationship with the agency Chief AI Officer and read the AI Action Plan. (ECQ5 Achieving Results)
