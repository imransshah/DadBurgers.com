## Three budgets at once

At any moment you are **executing** this fiscal year, **defending** next year's request, and **formulating** the year after.

| When | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| Oct 1 | Fiscal year starts (often under a CR) |
| Spring | Agency formulation for FY+2 |
| Late summer / Sept | Agency submission to OMB, with IT exhibits |
| Fall | OMB passback; appeals |
| Early Feb (target) | President's Budget released |
| Spring–Sept | Congressional hearings and appropriations |

## IT exhibits

- **Agency IT Portfolio Summary** (formerly "Exhibit 53"): every IT investment and its spend.
- **Major IT Business Case** (formerly "Exhibit 300"): detail for major investments; feeds the IT Dashboard.

## CPIC in one line each

- **Select**: is this the right investment?
- **Control**: is it on cost, schedule and performance?
- **Evaluate**: did it deliver what we said it would?

## Under a continuing resolution

- Generally funded at the prior-year rate.
- No new starts without specific authority. Plan procurements so they don't land in Q1.
