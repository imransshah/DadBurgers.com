## Overview
A U.S. Office of Special Counsel senior counsel explains the Hatch Act's purpose, prohibitions and enforcement, and how it applies to senior executives. Career SES members are further restricted in every agency. Political neutrality, the speaker stresses, is not policy neutrality.

## Key points
- Aims: nonpartisan administration, protection from political coercion at work, and merit-based advancement. [01:09]
- Prohibited: political activity on duty or in federal space, soliciting or receiving contributions, using authority to affect elections, partisan candidacy, and soliciting or discouraging political activity by people with business before the agency. [02:10]
- OSC advises, investigates and seeks discipline at MSPB; MSPB decides violations and penalties; OPM issues regulations [03:14]. Penalties run up to removal, debarment and a civil fine. [04:16]
- Career SES members are further restricted in every agency [10:24]. They may not share campaign posts [11:27] or help organize fundraisers [20:42], but may post their own views off duty. [12:27]
- Issue discussion is allowed [13:29]; on duty includes telework in paid status. [14:30]
- The fundraising ban applies at all times [18:37], with no family-and-friends exception. [19:40]
- The supervisor–subordinate relationship is inherently coercive [22:45]; in two cited cases, supervisors who pressed staff lost their jobs. [23:47]
- OSC and MSPB weigh the speaker–listener relationship, so SES members may face greater penalties. [34:00]

## Executive takeaways
- Treat any political request to a subordinate as off-limits. (ECQ4 Leading People)
- Career SES members face the strictest tier in every agency, including limits on off-duty campaign work that non-SES colleagues do not have. (ECQ1 Rule of Law)
- The on-duty ban covers email, social media, text and virtual chat; several cited suspensions involved on-duty Facebook or X posts. (ECQ1 Rule of Law)
- The speaker says leaders shape office rhetoric and culture, and what they allow has a downstream effect. (ECQ4 Leading People)
