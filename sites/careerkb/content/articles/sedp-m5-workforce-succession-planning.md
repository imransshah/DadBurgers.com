## Overview
A two-part conversation between host Dr. Rebecca Ayers and Jason Parman, Principal Deputy Associate Director for HR Solutions at OPM. Part 1 covers workforce planning, skills gaps and workload; Part 2 covers succession, incentives and measuring success. Timestamps restart in each part.

## Key points
- Part 1: Workforce planning is a repeating cycle of demand, supply, gaps, closing or restructuring, and measurement. It starts with demand: what the mission and administration priorities require. [03:09]
- Part 1: A skills gap analysis combines supervisor views, employee self-evaluations and organizational evidence such as position descriptions and performance metrics. Self-evaluations often surface hidden skills. [13:23]
- Part 1: With flat headcount, the speakers recommend targeting training funds at identified gaps, peer-to-peer teaching, and desktop AI tools used by knowledgeable staff. [24:38]
- Part 1: Training should be measured beyond attendance and learning: later use on the job, then organizational results. [33:46]
- Part 1: Workload analysis often finds duplication, excess management layers and stovepiped processes before a true staffing shortage. Sustained overtime is a real signal of need. [39:49]
- Part 2: Succession planning builds depth in critical and mission-critical positions. Parman stresses it is not preselection: leaders develop a candidate pool rather than naming a successor. [03:09]
- Part 2: An effective model matched business need, skills and employee interest to reassignments on a set cadence. [10:22]
- Part 2: Recruitment, relocation and retention incentives, tied to service agreements, are the appropriate way to address pay for hard-to-fill roles; over-grading positions is not. [16:33]
- Part 2: Success is measured by mission outcomes; counts of positions reclassified or people trained are not enough. [25:46]

## Executive takeaways
- Treat succession planning as merit-based bench building across critical roles, including technical operations staff. (ECQ3 Merit & Competence)
- Test structure, process and workload before requesting more staff. (ECQ2 Driving Efficiency)
- Tie every training, hiring and reorganization action to a mission outcome and report the outcome. (ECQ5 Achieving Results)
