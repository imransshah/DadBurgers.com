## The three levers

- **Budget**: the CIO has a significant role in planning and approving IT resources in the agency budget request.
- **Acquisition**: the CIO reviews and approves IT contracts and agreements (or delegates under a documented process).
- **Workforce**: the CIO is involved in hiring and evaluating component-level IT leadership.

## Common Baseline, in plain terms

OMB's implementing guidance turned the statute into a **Common Baseline** of roles and responsibilities that each agency self-assesses and documents. If your delegation or governance board doesn't trace back to it, expect questions.

## What oversight looks at

- Incremental delivery of major investments
- Data center optimization and cloud adoption
- Software license management
- CIO reporting relationship to the agency head
- Cybersecurity (FISMA) results

## Deputy CIO angle

- Keep the delegation memo current and findable.
- Know which contracts came to the CIO for review this fiscal year, and which didn't.
