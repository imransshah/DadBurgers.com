## Overview
Noah Peters, senior advisor to the OPM Director, explains the workforce orders under four pillars: return to office, a high-performance culture, restructuring, and stronger conduct standards. The session has two parts, marked pt 1 and pt 2.

## Key points
- Per Peters, EO 14151 ends federal DEI programs and EO 14173 bases hiring and promotion on merit, not DEIA [pt 1, 04:02]
- Return to office presumes five days on site, with exceptions such as reasonable accommodation, hardship and military spouses [pt 1, 13:12]
- Ratings center on level 3 [pt 1, 07:04]; supervisors and SES get accountability elements, and executives justify rating distributions [pt 1, 15:14]
- SES and senior professional level 4–5 ratings are initially capped at 30% (cadres of five or more) [pt 1, 20:19]
- Civil Service Rule 11 sets one-year probation (competitive) and two-year trial periods (excepted) [pt 1, 25:26], ending in an affirmative decision [pt 1, 26:26]
- EO 14210 asks agencies to rethink structure and whether each position is needed [pt 2, 01:00]; with the hiring freeze and merit hiring EO, Peters says hiring goes only to highest-need areas with a written business case [pt 2, 03:03]
- Schedule Policy/Career covers confidential and policy-determining, -making or -advocating roles, with faster removal [pt 2, 07:05], generally GS-11 and above [pt 2, 08:06]
- The Merit Hiring Plan requires skills-based assessments [pt 2, 10:09] and replaces SES ECQ narratives with a 2-page resume and structured interview [pt 2, 12:11]
- A suitability memorandum directs OPM rulemaking so post-appointment conduct can support a suitability removal, on agency-head referral [pt 2, 21:22]; EO 14251 excludes many agencies from collective bargaining [pt 2, 26:26]

## Executive takeaways
- Peters says supervisors and executives will be rated on holding employees accountable (ECQ4 Leading People)
- The Merit Hiring Plan shifts assessment from self-ratings to demonstrated skills (ECQ3 Merit & Competence)
- Peters says at least one performance element should reflect a Trump administration priority or an organizational goal (ECQ5 Achieving Results)
