## Overview
Noah Peters, senior advisor to the OPM Director, explains the workforce orders under four pillars: return to office, a high-performance culture, restructuring, and stronger conduct standards. The session has two parts, marked pt 1 and pt 2.

## Key points
- EO 14151 and EO 14173 end DEI positions and non-merit factors in hiring and promotion [pt 1, 04:02]
- Return to office presumes five days on site, with exceptions such as reasonable accommodation, hardship and military spouses [pt 1, 13:12]
- Ratings center on level 3; supervisors and SES get accountability elements; executives justify rating distributions [pt 1, 15:14]
- SES and senior professional level 4–5 ratings are initially capped at 30% (cadres of five or more) [pt 1, 20:19]
- Civil Service Rule 11 sets one-year probation (competitive) and two-year trial periods (excepted), ending in an affirmative decision [pt 1, 24:24]
- EO 14210 limits hiring to the highest-need areas, backed by a written business case [pt 2, 01:00]
- Schedule Policy/Career covers confidential and policy-determining, -making or -advocating roles, generally GS-11 and above, with faster removal [pt 2, 06:05]
- The Merit Hiring Plan requires skills-based assessments and replaces SES ECQ narratives with a 2-page resume and structured interview [pt 2, 10:09]
- EO 14251 excludes many agencies from collective bargaining; a suitability memorandum allows removal for post-appointment conduct [pt 2, 26:26]

## Executive takeaways
- Supervising performance and conduct is now a rated duty (ECQ4 Leading People)
- Assess candidates on demonstrated skills (ECQ3 Merit & Competence)
- Link at least one element in each plan to an organizational goal (ECQ5 Achieving Results)
