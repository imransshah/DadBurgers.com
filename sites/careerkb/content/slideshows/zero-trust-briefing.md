# Zero trust, in seven slides

Leadership briefing · placeholder deck

???
Open with the one-liner: we stop trusting the network and start verifying every request.

---

## Why it matters

- The perimeter is gone: cloud, mobile, telework, partners
- Most breaches move laterally after one stolen credential
- Zero trust limits blast radius: **never trust, always verify**

---

## Where the requirement comes from

- **EO 14028** set the direction (May 2021)
- **OMB M-22-09** set the federal strategy and goals (Jan 2022)
- **CISA ZTMM v2.0** is the yardstick for maturity
